# Security Policy

Do not report leaked hidden cases, gold answers, scorer code, credentials, identifiable records, or participant secrets in a public issue.

Public reports may describe the affected release, exposure class, and remediation status without reproducing the sensitive material. Maintainers should treat evaluator extraction, answer-bearing fixtures, adaptive leakage through diagnostics, and repository-history exposure as security events. Affected sealed sets may require retirement; deletion alone does not restore cleanliness.
